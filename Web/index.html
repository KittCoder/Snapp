Welcome to SNAPP

Build balanced meals with clarity. SNAPP helps you plan your week, understand nutrition foundations, and turn food choices into habits that are realistic for your schedule.

Core Food Groups

A balanced plate generally includes complex carbohydrates, lean proteins, healthy fats, fiber-rich produce, and hydration support. The goal is not perfection at every meal, but consistency across the week.

Vegetables and Fruits

Rich in vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and fiber. Prioritize color variety to broaden nutrient coverage and support long-term health.

Whole Grains and Smart Carbs

Oats, brown rice, quinoa, and whole-grain breads provide steady energy and help maintain focus through the day.

Protein Sources

Fish, poultry, legumes, tofu, eggs, and Greek yogurt support muscle repair, satiety, and stable appetite control.

Healthy Fats

Avocado, nuts, seeds, olive oil, and fatty fish support hormone function and improve absorption of fat-soluble vitamins.

Simple Plate Method

Use this quick visual method when planning lunches and dinners: half your plate vegetables, one quarter lean protein, and one quarter complex carbohydrates.

Add a small serving of healthy fat and keep water nearby. This structure helps improve fullness, nutrient diversity, and energy consistency without counting every macro.

Weekly Nutrition Focus

Instead of overhauling everything at once, anchor one weekly focus: add one vegetable to breakfast, include protein at every meal, or swap refined snacks for fiber-rich options. Small repeated changes create durable progress.

Fiber Target

Include produce, beans, whole grains, and seeds throughout the day to support digestion and appetite control.

Hydration

Build a routine with water at meals and between tasks to support concentration and overall performance.

Protein Distribution

Spread protein across breakfast, lunch, and dinner for better satiety and recovery than loading it into one meal.

Use SNAPP Throughout Your Day

Morning: verify your meals and prep ingredients. Midday: ask the chatbot for substitutions based on what you have. Evening: review tomorrow's plan and update preferences so the schedule keeps adapting with you.
